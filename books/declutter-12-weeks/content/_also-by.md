# Also by Emily P. Harper

*House Cleaning Checklist Planner*

Once your rooms are clear, this planner is what keeps them that way. It picks up where Part 4 leaves off, with weekly and monthly cleaning checklists and home routines in short blocks you can fit into a busy week.

More books in this series are on the way, each one focused on a single part of the home: closets, the kitchen, the garage, paperwork, and downsizing to a smaller place.
