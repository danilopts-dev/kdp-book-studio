# Your Printable Declutter Kit

Some pages work better on a wall than inside a book. The Printable Declutter Kit is a free PDF with the sheets I wanted taped up where I could see them while I worked, so you can print them as often as you need.

What's in the kit:

- **A Keep / Donate / Sell / Toss flowchart.** The same tie-breaker from Week 0, laid out as a one-page chart you can stick inside a closet door and check while your hands are full.
- **Room checklists to print again.** One per room, so you have a clean copy for each monthly 15-minute reset in Part 4 instead of writing over last month's marks.
- **A 12-week tracker for the fridge door.** Check off each week as you finish it. Everyone in the house can see how far you've come, and nobody has to open the book to find out.
