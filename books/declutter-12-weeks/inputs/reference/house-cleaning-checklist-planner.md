Weekly Checklist
WEEKLY CHECKLIST GUIDE
This section helps you keep chaos away and your home sparkling with zero overwhelm, in
only 15 minutes a day.
How to use it (4 simple steps):
1. Everyday Habits:
Check each quick task as you do it, once per day.
These actions reset your home daily.
P.S. It’s OK if you miss them occasionally — just pick up again the next day!
2. Day-By-Day Focus:
Follow the themed tasks for each day (Mon–Sun).
Check as you complete them.
Add your own in the blank lines if needed.
3. Deep Clean Goals:
Write up to 3 bigger tasks for the week (pull from Monthly Focus or your own list).
Break them into your Saturday/Sunday slots.
4. Notes + Wins:
Write down what worked, what didn’t, or quick discoveries (e.g., “Found lost sock!”).
Celebrate: “Whole house smells fresh!”
After finishing the week:
→ Feel the win of a clean, calm week!
→ Flip to the next page and start fresh.
“7 small days = 1 big clean home”

Declutter Challenge
RECLAIM YOUR SPACE
Welcome to your Declutter Challenge.
Getting started can feel overwhelming. Clutter isn’t just about "stuff." It is about the
energy and time you spend managing things you no longer need. This section provides a
complete roadmap to lighten your load, both physically and digitally.
In this section, you will find four powerful tools designed to transform your home step-by-
step:
30-Day Declutter Challenge: Build the momentum. A daily guide to letting go of
obvious clutter and creating a habit of detachment.
14-Day Digital Declutter: Clear your virtual space. A two-week focused plan to
organize emails, photos, and files for a sharper mind.
One Room Per Month Challenge: Deep focus. Dedicate one month to mastering a
specific area of your home, ensuring no corner is left behind.
Declutter Log: Celebrate the exit. Track every item you sell, donate, or discard, and
watch the weight lift off your shoulders.

TIME-SAVING HACKS
Cleaning doesn't have to consume your whole day. Use these efficiency strategies to
speed up your routine and get better results with less effort.
1. The "Top-to-Bottom" Rule - Gravity is your friend. Always clean the highest points first
(shelves, fans, cabinets) and work your way down. This way, dust falls onto the floor,
which you vacuum last. If you clean the floor first, you’ll just dirty it again.
2. The Laundry Basket Method - Never leave a room to put an item away while cleaning
since it wastes time walking back and forth. Instead, take a laundry basket into the room.
Put everything that doesn't belong there into the basket. Once the room is clean,
distribute the basket items in one go.
3. Dry First, Wet Second - Never spray cleaner on a dusty surface. The liquid turns dust
into mud, which is harder to wipe up. Always dust or vacuum the surface dry first, then
apply your spray cleaner for the finish.
4. The "Task Batching" Technique - Stop cleaning room-by-room (completing the
kitchen, then moving to the bathroom). Instead, clean task-by-task through the whole
house. Dust every room first, then clean all mirrors, then vacuum everywhere. You waste
much less time switching tools and mental gears.
5. Carry Your Kit (The Caddy System) - Don’t run back to the cupboard for every
different bottle. Keep a portable caddy with your essentials (all-purpose spray, glass
cleaner, cloths, sponge, trash bag). Carry it from room to room like a pro.
6. The 2-Minute Rule - If a task takes less than 2 minutes (e.g., wiping the sink, putting
shoes in the closet, hanging a coat), do it immediately. Do not add it to a to-do list.
7. Let the Product Do the Work - Spray your surfaces (sink, toilet, stove) and walk away
for 10 minutes. Go do something else. When you come back, the chemistry has broken
down the dirt, and you can wipe it off without scrubbing.
Pro Tip: Cleaning feels faster when your mind is distracted. Designate a specific podcast
or audiobook series that you are ONLY allowed to listen to while cleaning. It turns chore
time into "me time."

15 MINUTE SPEED CLEAN PROTOCOL
Designed for when the doorbell is about to ring and chaos reigns. This emergency guide
skips the deep cleaning to focus strictly on high-traffic areas and visible clutter, helping
you fake a perfectly tidy home before the timer runs out. Engage in these rules:
Set a Timer: You must race against the clock.
No Deep Cleaning: Do not scrub grout. Do not organize inside drawers.
No Distractions: Do not check your phone.
MINUTES 00–03: THE GATHERING (Clear Surfaces)
Tool: Laundry Basket + Trash Bag.
Trash Walk: Do a lap of the main rooms. Throw away all obvious trash.
The Basket Sweep: Grab the laundry basket. Sweep all items that don't belong in the
living room/kitchen (shoes, toys, mail) into the basket. Do not put them away yet.
Hide the basket in a closet/bedroom.
MINUTES 03–08: KITCHEN & LIVING RESET
Tool: All-Purpose Spray + Microfiber Cloth.
Dishes: Load dirty dishes into the dishwasher. If full, stack them neatly in the sink (or
hide inside the oven if desperate!).
Wipe Down: Spray and wipe kitchen counters and the dining table.
The "Fluff": Straighten sofa pillows and fold throw blankets. A tidy sofa makes the
whole room look cleaner.
MINUTES 08–12: THE BATHROOM RESCUE
Tool: Disinfectant Wipe + Fresh Towel.
Counter & Mirror: Wipe toothpaste splatters off the mirror and sink.
Toilet: Quick wipe of the seat and rim. Flush.
Shower: Close the shower curtain (hides the mess inside).
Towels: Swap the dirty hand towel for a fresh, folded one.
MINUTES 12–15: FLOORS & ATMOSPHERE
Tool: Vacuum/Broom + Candle.
Spot Clean: Only vacuum or sweep visible crumbs in high-traffic areas (entryway,
kitchen rug). Don't do the whole house.
The Vibe: Dim the lights (hides dust). Light a scented candle or spray room spray.
FINAL CHECK: Take a deep breath. You made it!
