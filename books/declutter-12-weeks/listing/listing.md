## Descrição (HTML para o KDP)

```html
<p><b>You don't need a free weekend or a garage full of bins. You need 15 minutes and a pencil.</b></p>

<p>Most decluttering plans start with a big push: pull everything out, sort it all, finish by Sunday. Then Sunday ends, the bed is still covered in clothes, and half of it goes back in the closet. This workbook takes the slower road on purpose. You spend 15 minutes a day deciding about the things in one room, and you move to the next room each week. Twelve weeks later, you have been through every room in the house.</p>

<p><b>Every item gets one of four answers.</b> Keep, Donate, Sell or Toss. There is no "maybe" box. When you're stuck, one question settles it: if this were gone tomorrow, would you go out and buy it again? Before you start, you also write down your own sell threshold, the dollar amount that decides whether something is worth listing or should simply go to a donation center. After the first week or two, most decisions take a few seconds.</p>

<p><b>The order of the rooms is the plan.</b> You start with the bathroom, a small room with a door you can close, where almost nothing carries a memory. You build up through the kitchen and the closet to the paper pile in the home office, and you save sentimental things for the last week, when you have made hundreds of easier decisions and finally have the space to think about the hard ones.</p>

<p><b>Inside you'll find:</b></p>
<p>
&bull; A Week 0 walk-through where you rate every room from 1 to 5 and take "before" photos<br>
&bull; A 7-day, 15-minute task list for each of the 12 weeks, with a checkbox for every day<br>
&bull; Fill-in pages built for each room: the reverse-hanger test for the closet, a document-keeping guide for the paper pile, a toy rotation plan for kids' rooms<br>
&bull; A weekly Keep / Donate / Sell / Toss count, so you can see exactly what left the house<br>
&bull; A Sell Tracker and a Donation Log, with space for asking price, sold price and fair market value<br>
&bull; A monthly 15-minute reset tracker and a one in, one out log to keep the rooms clear afterward<br>
&bull; A before-and-after scoreboard: items out, money earned, value donated, hours invested
</p>

<p>This book won't teach you to organize or tell you to buy containers. It is about deciding, which is what actually clears a room. Once most of the extra is gone, what's left is easy to put away.</p>

<p><b>Made for real homes.</b> 8.5 x 11 inches with room to write, undated so you can start on any day, and written for people who are tired by eight at night and still want a clear house.</p>

<p>When the clutter is gone, the <i>House Cleaning Checklist Planner</i> by Emily P. Harper picks up where this book leaves off, with routines to keep each room clean in the same short blocks.</p>

<p><b>Grab a pencil and start with Week 0. You don't have to touch anything yet.</b></p>
```

## A+ Content — plano de módulos (970 x 600)

**Módulo 1 — Hero**
- Visual: capa à esquerda; à direita, foto de uma bancada de cozinha vazia com uma xícara e um lápis sobre o livro aberto.
- Headline: *Fifteen minutes a day. One room a week. The whole house in 12 weeks.*
- Body: A fill-in workbook that walks you through every room in order, from the bathroom to the box of old letters, one small decision at a time.

**Módulo 2 — Problema e contexto**
- Visual: closet aberto, cheio, com um único cabide virado ao contrário em destaque.
- Headline: *The weekend purge didn't work. Here's what does.*
- Body: Big cleanouts burn out by Sunday afternoon. This plan asks for 15 minutes, a pencil and four boxes you already have. You decide about one drawer or one shelf at a time, and the decisions get faster every week.

**Módulo 3 — Por dentro do livro**
- Visual: 3 páginas reais do miolo, lado a lado: abertura de semana (Week 7 — The Closet, com as tarefas diárias), Keep / Donate / Sell / Toss Count, Sell Tracker.
- Headline: *Every week has a plan, and every page has room to write.*
- Bullets: 7 daily 15-minute tasks per room · Room-specific worksheets (reverse-hanger test, paper-keeping guide, toy rotation) · Weekly count of what left the house · Sell Tracker and Donation Log

**Módulo 4 — Comparação e valor**
- Visual: duas colunas. À esquerda, este livro com checkmarks; à direita, um livro genérico sem marca, com itens esmaecidos.
- Headline: *Built for deciding, not for buying more storage.*
- Ours: Room order from easiest to hardest, sentimental last · A tie-breaker question and your own sell threshold · Tracks money earned and value donated · Monthly reset to keep it clear
- Generic: Pretty storage ideas · Same checklist for every room · No record of what you sold or gave away

**Módulo 5 — Fechamento**
- Visual: o mesmo cômodo do módulo 2, agora arrumado, e o livro aberto na página "Your Before and After".
- Headline: *See the before and after in your own handwriting.*
- Body: Week 0 gives you a score for every room. Week 12 gives you a new one, plus the totals: items out, money earned, value donated. Then the *House Cleaning Checklist Planner* takes it from there.
