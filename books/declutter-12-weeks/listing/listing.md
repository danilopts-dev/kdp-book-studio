## Descrição (HTML para o KDP)

```html
<p><b>Set a timer for 15 minutes, pick up a pencil, and start with the bathroom.</b></p>

<p>Most decluttering plans start with a big push: pull everything out, sort it all, finish by Sunday. Then Sunday ends, the bed is still covered in clothes, and half of it goes back in the closet. This workbook takes the slower road on purpose. Each day you decide about the things in one small part of one room, and each week you move on to the next room. Twelve weeks later, you have been through the whole house.</p>

<p><b>Every item goes into one of four boxes.</b> Keep it, donate it, sell it or toss it. There is no "maybe" box. When you're stuck, one question settles it: if this were gone tomorrow, would you go out and buy it again? Before you start, you also write down your own sell threshold, the dollar amount that decides whether something is worth listing or should simply go to a donation center. Many decisions get faster once you've made a few dozen of them.</p>

<p><b>The order of the rooms is the plan.</b> You start with the bathroom, a small room with a door you can close, where almost nothing carries a memory. You work up through the kitchen and the closet to the paper pile in the home office, and you save sentimental things for the last week, when you have made hundreds of easier decisions and finally have the space to think about the hard ones.</p>

<p><b>Inside you'll find:</b></p>
<p>
&bull; A Week 0 walk-through where you rate every room from 1 to 5 and take "before" photos<br>
&bull; A 7-day task list for each of the 12 weeks, 15 minutes a day, with a checkbox for every day<br>
&bull; Fill-in pages built for each room, such as the reverse-hanger test for the closet and a toy rotation plan for kids' rooms<br>
&bull; A weekly count of what you kept, donated, sold and tossed, so you can see exactly what left the house<br>
&bull; A Sell Tracker and a Donation Log, with space for asking price, sold price and fair market value<br>
&bull; A monthly 15-minute reset tracker and a one in, one out log to keep the rooms clear afterward<br>
&bull; A before-and-after scoreboard: items out, money earned, value donated, hours invested
</p>

<p>There is no organizing system to learn and no containers to buy. The book is about deciding, which is what actually clears a room, and once most of the extra is gone, what's left is easy to put away.</p>

<p><b>Made for real homes.</b> 8.5 x 11 inches with room to write, undated so you can start on any day, and written for people who are tired by eight at night and still want a clear house.</p>

<p>When the clutter is gone, the <i>House Cleaning Checklist Planner</i> by Emily P. Harper picks up where this book leaves off, with weekly and monthly routines to keep each room clean.</p>

<p><b>Start with Week 0 whenever you're ready. You don't have to touch anything yet.</b></p>
```

Auditoria human-voice (2026-10-06):
- Negação-correção: uma só ("There is no organizing system to learn and no containers to buy"). O gancho antigo "You don't need X. You need Y." foi trocado por uma instrução concreta (timer, lápis, banheiro).
- Sem vocabulário proibido (journey, transform, unlock, empower etc.), sem travessões, sem sequência de mais de 2 frases curtas, tríades só onde a lista é real (quatro caixas, quatro totais). A lista de recursos foi reduzida de três exemplos para dois.
- Variação em relação ao subtítulo: "Keep, Donate, Sell or Toss" virou "keep it, donate it, sell it or toss it" dentro de uma frase; "No Storage Bins" virou "no containers to buy".
- Removido "After the first week or two, most decisions take a few seconds" (promessa que o livro não mede) e "in the same short blocks" (não verificado no livro irmão). Sem claim médico e sem promessa de dedução fiscal; o texto fala em "fair market value".
- Todos os itens da lista de conteúdo conferem com o miolo (wk00, wk07, wk08, part3, part4, conclusion).
- O bônus (Printable Declutter Kit) não é citado na descrição enquanto o PDF não existir (pendência em questions.md).

## A+ Content — plano de módulos (970 x 600)

Plano de 5 módulos (Hero, Problema/Contexto, Inside Pages Preview, Comparação e Valor, Fechamento). Aguarda aprovação do Danilo.

**Módulo 1 — Hero**
- Visual: capa à esquerda; à direita, foto de uma bancada de cozinha vazia com uma xícara e um lápis sobre o livro aberto.
- Headline: *Clear the whole house in 12 weeks, fifteen minutes at a time.*
- Body: A fill-in workbook that walks you through every room in order, from the bathroom to the box of old letters, one small decision at a time.

**Módulo 2 — Problema e contexto**
- Visual: closet aberto, cheio, com um único cabide virado ao contrário em destaque.
- Headline: *Why the weekend purge never lasts past Sunday.*
- Body: Big cleanouts run out of steam by Sunday afternoon. This plan asks for 15 minutes, a pencil and a few boxes or bags you already have. You decide about one drawer or one shelf at a time, and the decisions get quicker as the weeks go on.

**Módulo 3 — Por dentro do livro (Inside Pages Preview)**
- Visual: 3 páginas reais do miolo, lado a lado: abertura de semana (Week 7, The Closet, com as tarefas diárias), Keep / Donate / Sell / Toss Count, Sell Tracker.
- Headline: *Every week has a plan, and every page has room to write.*
- Bullets: 7 daily 15-minute tasks per room · Room-specific worksheets, like the reverse-hanger test and the toy rotation plan · Weekly count of what left the house · Sell Tracker and Donation Log

**Módulo 4 — Comparação e valor**
- Visual: duas colunas. À esquerda, este livro com checkmarks; à direita, um livro genérico sem marca, com itens esmaecidos.
- Headline: *Twelve weeks of deciding, with no containers to buy.*
- Ours: Room order from easiest to hardest, sentimental last · A tie-breaker question and your own sell threshold · Tracks money earned and value donated · Monthly reset to keep it clear
- Generic: Pretty storage ideas · Same checklist for every room · No record of what you sold or gave away

**Módulo 5 — Fechamento**
- Visual: o mesmo cômodo do módulo 2, agora arrumado, e o livro aberto na página "Your Before and After".
- Headline: *See the before and after in your own handwriting.*
- Body: You rate every room in Week 0 and again in Week 12, and the last pages add up your totals: items out, money earned, value donated. After that, the *House Cleaning Checklist Planner* takes over the upkeep.
