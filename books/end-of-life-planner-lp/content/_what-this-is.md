# What This Book Is, and What It Isn't

This book is a place to write down two kinds of things: what you want, and where your things are. When someone has to step in for you, they can find the answers in your own handwriting instead of guessing.

## What it does

- Lists the people to call, and in what order.
- Shows where your papers, accounts, policies, keys, and pets' records are kept.
- Records your health details, your care wishes, and how you'd like your funeral or memorial done.
- Gives you room for a few personal words, if you want to leave them.

## What it doesn't do

Nothing you write here is a legal document. It can't take the place of a will, a trust, a power of attorney, an advance directive, or a living will, and it doesn't give legal, financial, or medical advice. For those papers, or for questions about money, taxes, or your health, ask an attorney, an accountant, or your doctor, then write down here where the signed papers are kept.

## One rule for every page

Never write a password or a full account, card, or Social Security number in this book. A book can be lost or read by the wrong person. Wherever a number would go, write where it's kept instead, or the last four digits if you'd like.
