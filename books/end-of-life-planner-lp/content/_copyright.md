This book is a personal organizer for writing down your wishes and where your things are kept. It does not give legal, financial, tax, or medical advice, and nothing written in it creates a will, trust, power of attorney, advance directive, or any other legal document. For those, please talk with a licensed attorney, accountant, or doctor where you live.

Do not write passwords or full account, card, or Social Security numbers in this book.
