# Start Here: Four Weekends

You don't have to do this all at once. The chapters are grouped into four weekends, and the first one covers what your family would need in an emergency, so even if you stop there, the most urgent pages are done.

A weekend is only a way to break the work up. Take two if you need them, or skip a month between them. Leave a blank where you don't know an answer yet, and come back once you've found the paper or made the phone call. Pencil is fine.

## Weekend 1: What Someone Needs in an Emergency

- The One Page Your Family Will Read First
- Who to Call, and in What Order
- Where My Important Papers Are
- What a Doctor Needs to Know About Me in an Emergency

## When You're Done

Tell at least one person where this book is kept, and write their name on the "Where This Book Is Kept" page at the front. Once a year, look it over and update what has changed.
