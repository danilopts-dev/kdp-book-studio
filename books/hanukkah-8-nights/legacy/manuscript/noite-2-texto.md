### Tonight's Story (≈150 palavras)

After Judah and his brothers won battle after battle, they finally climbed the steps of the Temple in Jerusalem, the holiest place their people had. They expected to find it standing tall and proud. Instead, they found a mess.

The king's soldiers had broken the altar, smashed the jars, and set up idols in the place where their people had always prayed. Dust covered everything. Cobwebs hung in corners that used to shine.

Judah didn't sit down and cry about it. He rolled up his sleeves. "We came back for this place," he said, "so let's give it back its life." Together, the Maccabees swept the floors, carried out the broken pieces, and scrubbed until their arms ached.

By the end of the day, the Temple wasn't perfect yet. But it was theirs again, and it was clean enough to hope in.

### What's a menorah vs. a hanukkiah? (caixa, ≤40 palavras)

**WHAT'S A MENORAH VS. A HANUKKIAH?**

The Temple menorah had seven branches and burned every day. A hanukkiah, also called a Hanukkah menorah, has nine: eight for the eight nights, plus the shamash, a helper candle that lights the others.

**HOW IS IT DIFFERENT FROM THE HANUKKIAH?**

| | Temple menorah | Hanukkiah (Hanukkah menorah) |
|---|---|---|
| Lights | 7 (an oil lamp on each branch) | 9 candle holders (8 Hanukkah lights + 1 shamash) |
| Lit | Every day, as part of the Temple service | One more candle each of the 8 nights |
| Job of the extra candle | No extra candle | The shamash lights all the others |

Some count me at seven, some count me at nine,

What am I?
