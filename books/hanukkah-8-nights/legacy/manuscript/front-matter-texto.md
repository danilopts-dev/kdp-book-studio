### How This Book Works (2 páginas)

**PAGE 1**

**HOW THIS BOOK WORKS**

Welcome! This book has one part for each of the eight nights of Hanukkah. Read one night at a time, right before you light the candles, and you're done for the day. Most nights take about 20 to 30 minutes from start to finish.

You don't have to start on Night 1. If tonight is the third night of Hanukkah, turn to Night 3 and start there. Every night stands on its own, so nobody is behind.

Each night has two levels of activities. Look for the stars next to the title:

**★** activities are for younger puzzle solvers, around ages 6 to 7.
**★★** activities are for kids who want more of a challenge, around ages 8 to 10.

Nobody has to pick just one level. A seven year old who loves a challenge can try the ★★ page, and an older kid having an off night can go back to ★. The stars are a starting point, not a rule.

Most of the story and the "Before the Candles" page are for reading together as a family. The puzzle pages are built for a kid to do mostly on their own, with a grown up nearby to help if a word is tricky or a maze gets frustrating.

**PAGE 2**

**LIGHTING THE HANUKKIAH**

Every family does this a little differently, and that's fine. Here's one way that works:

1. Place the candles in the hanukkiah first, adding one candle each night, starting from the right.
2. Say the blessings before you light anything.
3. Use the shamash, the "helper candle," to light the others. The shamash isn't one of the eight lights. It's the one that does the lighting.
4. Light the newest candle first, moving from left to right, then put the shamash back in its own spot.

Families have different customs about exactly when to light the shamash and when to say the blessings. Follow your family's way. This book's puzzles and stories work no matter which order you use.

Most families put the hanukkiah somewhere it can be seen from outside, like a window facing the street, since sharing the light is part of the holiday.
